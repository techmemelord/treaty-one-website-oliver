What Indigenous Nations were involved?

There were lots of Indigenous people involved in the signing of Treaty 1, but first, lets know what Indigenous Nations were involved in the signing of Treaty 1.

Below is a list of the Indigenous Nations involved in Treaty 1:

  • Anishinaabe (also known as the Ojibwe people)
  • Swampy Cree

What communties in those nations were involved?

Considering now that we know what Indigenous Nations were involved, we will now look at what communties were involved.

Below is a list of what Indigenous communties were involved in Treaty 1:

  • Brokenhead
  • Sagkeeng
  • Long Plain
  • Peguis
  • Roseau River
  • Sandy Bay
  • Swan Lake

What people were involved from those communties and what Nation were they from?

since we have now specified up to what communties have had some involvement or signficance for the negotiations and signing of Treaty 1, you may want to know who in particular was a part of this process and what Nation they are from.

  • Whip-poor-will (Wa-ko-wush) - Nation: Unknown
