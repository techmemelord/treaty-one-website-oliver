What Indigenous Nations were involved in Treaty 1?

Many Indigenous peoples were involved in the signing of Treaty 1.

The Indigenous Nations involved in Treaty 1 were:

Anishinaabe (Ojibwe People)

The Anishinaabe, also known as the Ojibwe people, were one of the main Indigenous groups involved in Treaty 1. They have a strong history and culture connected to Manitoba and other parts of Canada.

Swampy Cree

The Swampy Cree were another major Indigenous group involved in Treaty 1. Their communities have lived in Manitoba for many generations and continue to be an important part of Treaty 1 today.

What communities in those Nations were involved?

Now that we know the Indigenous Nations involved in Treaty 1, we can look at the First Nations communities connected to the treaty.

The Treaty 1 First Nations communities include:

Brokenhead

Sagkeeng

Long Plain

Peguis

Roseau River

Sandy Bay

Swan Lake

These communities are mainly part of the Anishinaabe (Ojibwe) and Cree Nations.Peguis First Nation includes both Ojibwe and Cree heritage.

What people were involved from those communties and what Nation were they from?

Since we have now identified which communities were involved in the negotiations and signing of Treaty 1, you may want to know which specific people were part of this process and what Nations they were from.

Below is a list of the percise people that had involvement in Treaty 1 and the Indigenous Nations or communties they're from:

  • Red Eagle (Mis-koo-ke-new, or Henry Prince) - Nation: Peguis
  • Bird Forever (Ka-ke-ka-penais, or William Pennefather) - Nation: Sagkeeng
  • Flying Down Bird (Na-sha-ke-penais) - Nation: Brokenhead
  • Centre of Bird’s Tail (Na-na-wa-nanan) - Nation: Roseau River
  • Flying Round (Ke-we-tay-ash) - Nation: Unknown
  • Whip-poor-will (Wa-ko-wush) - Nation: Unknown
  • Yellow Quill (Os-za-we-kwun) - Nation: Unknown
