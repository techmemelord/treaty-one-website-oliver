What is Treaty 1?

Treaty 1 was the first numbered treaty that was signed in Canada on August 3, 1871. It covers land in southern Manitoba, including Winnipeg, and became the starting point for the other numbered treaties across Canada. This treaty was signed in the ″Stone Fort″ which is Lower Fort Garry in modern-day Manitoba. It is important because it is the treaty land that Winnipeg is located on today.

What Does a Treaty Mean?

A treaty was a written agreement between two Nations that was approved and signed by both of their leaders. In this case, a numbered treaty in Canada would be an agreement between a group of First Nations people or communities and Canada over a part of land. In exchange, these First Nations people will have land reserved for them which is called ″reserve land″.

Treaty 1 is located in southern Manitoba and is part of the numbered treaties across Canada.

Did You Know?

Treaty 1 was signed on August 3, 1871, at Lower Fort Garry in Manitoba.

What Makes this Treaty Unique from Others?

  • What makes this Treaty different from other Treaties in Canada is that it was the first Numbered Treaty signed in Canada.
  • This became the foundation for all the other treaties being signed and helped Canada learn how to make agreements with the First Nations people that were living on that land thousands of years before they came.
  • The treaties also established their sovereignty of certain parts of Canada that would have been taken over by other countries if those Treaties haven't been signed at all.
